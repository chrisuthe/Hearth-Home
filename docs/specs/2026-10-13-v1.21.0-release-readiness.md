Noted, not filed:

- A cast in portrait is cropped to a centre strip (`BoxFit.cover`) — already in the orientation spec's follow-ups, now seen on the tablet.
- The Pi drops video buffers on the 8 Mbit/s test clip on both bundles. It may be the clip; a real title was not compared.
