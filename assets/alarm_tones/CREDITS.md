# Alarm Tone Credits

The alarm tones bundled here are sourced from [Freesound.org](https://freesound.org/) under either Creative Commons Zero (CC0, public-domain-equivalent) or Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY) licenses. CC0 sounds need no credit; CC-BY sounds are credited below.

All files have been processed with `ffmpeg -af loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=11` for consistent broadcast-style loudness across the catalog, and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis (`-q:a 4`) for bundle-size reasons.
